# 🚀 CAREERGENIE – AI-POWERED CAREER INTELLIGENCE AND EMPLOYABILITY PLATFORM

CAREERGENIE is an AI-powered career intelligence and employability platform designed to help students and job seekers prepare for modern recruitment processes. It combines resume optimization, job analysis, skill-demand analysis, company requirement analysis, AI exams, mock interviews, career guidance, and employability assessment into a single platform.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

Students often use different platforms for resume building, job searching, skill analysis, interview preparation, and career guidance.

CAREERGENIE brings these activities together in one integrated platform.

The platform follows the complete career preparation journey:

**Resume → Jobs → Skills → Preparation → Interview → Assessment → Employability**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📄 AI Resume Builder
- Generate professional resumes
- ATS-friendly resume structure
- Resume optimization
- Job-specific keyword optimization
- A4 resume format
- Download resume as PDF

### 🔍 Resume Analyzer
- Analyze resume content
- Compare resume with Job Description
- Identify matching skills
- Identify missing skills
- Generate ATS and Job Match insights

### 💼 Job Intelligence
- Discover relevant job opportunities
- Display company and role information
- Show qualifications and required skills
- Provide job application links
- Support job-oriented career discovery

### 📊 Skill Demand Analyzer
- Analyze programming and technical skill demand
- Generate role-specific skills
- Display skill demand information
- Analyze trending skills
- Help students identify important technologies to learn

### 🏢 Company Requirement Analysis
- Analyze company-specific requirements
- Identify required technical skills
- Understand role expectations
- Support company-focused interview preparation

### 🤖 AI Career Chatbot
- Interactive career assistant
- Programming-related question answering
- Python, Java, C, C++, and SQL support
- AI-powered career guidance
- Learning assistance

### 📝 AI Exams
- Subject-based practice
- Multiple difficulty levels
- Technical practice questions
- Exam submission and evaluation
- Performance-based preparation

### 🎤 AI Mock Interview
- Practice interview questions
- Technical interview preparation
- HR interview preparation
- AI-based response evaluation
- Interview performance feedback

### 🧠 Personalized Interview Roadmap
- Identify preparation areas
- Find skill gaps
- Suggest learning priorities
- Organize interview preparation

### 📈 Employability Assessment
- Evaluate career preparation
- Analyze skills and interview performance
- Identify improvement areas
- Provide interview-readiness insights
